Disconnected from the grid, starved of the source code.

You cannot navigate the extreme chaos of the 21st century without an anchor. Religion offers comfort, science offers mechanisms, but civilization offers purpose. DODGIO exists to plug you back into the motherboard. You don’t need to hate your roots to love technology; your roots are the very blueprint of technology.
